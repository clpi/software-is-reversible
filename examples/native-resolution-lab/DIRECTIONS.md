## One concrete derived theorem target

Let `D(x)` be the set of admissible actions on fine interpretation `x`; a coarse observation `y` denotes one explicitly registered fiber `F(y)`. An action `a` is safe to select without further refinement exactly when `F(y)` is nonempty and `a ∈ ⋂_{x∈F(y)} D(x)`. This is an action-consensus certificate, not a claim that the latent intent itself was reconstructed. On a refined nonempty subfiber, its common admitted-action set can only grow. A single witnessed counterexample to admissibility blocks the action, even when all other witnesses agree.

For optimization, replace `D(x)` with the actions certified Pareto-undominated at `x`, keeping a common action identity and externally checked feasibility. The intersection rule does not mean one action optimizes every interpretation; an empty intersection is a precise refinement demand. Outcome probability, information removed by projection, and cost of the next observation are distinct axes. Enumerate and certify the bounded choices; measure any advantage of factoring rather than giving it a prior winner label.

## Admission for claims

A theorem proves its stated model. An independently replayed artifact tests implementation correspondence within its recorded input domain. A forecast remains a forecast until it has both an exact baseline and an observed result. Expand the search space append-only and issue a new certificate; do not upgrade an earlier optimum retroactively. Do not claim novelty because a prior-art cell is still unsearched.

## Recorded outcome from this cut

- The planner now decides feasibility, Pareto membership and a weighted winner in Idol, not in a foreign scorer. A browser snapshot changed from 40 resolved plans / 16 frontier vectors to 62 / 18 after appending one registered edge. That is an exact result within this graph, not an assertion about all future appends.
- The pure weight-only reranker agreed with complete re-solving and an independent oracle on 2,232 retunes; 420 changed the winner without modifying frontier bytes. A changed axis, profile or packet does not use the retained frontier. Compatible cross-cut reuse remains the next implementation, supported by the formal factor and receipt-lifting theorems.
- Flag provenance is missing. When a caller forges a declared proposal into a qualified edge, that action appears on the native frontier. The browser experiment is intentionally editable; production needs evidence-bound grants before this flag can be trusted.
- An independent compression investigation found 4,095 complete witnesses represented by 12 exact suffix classes on a 12-level repeated-pattern case. One-edit scopes left 4,064, 3,072 and 4,094 witnesses. Counts are not byte-size or latency claims. This is the next measurement target for compact, incrementally updated witness paging.

The native suffix page now reproduces all four 12-level controls and exactly counts 1,073,741,823 supported realizations from 30 states, while returning a requested page without materializing the rest. An independent oracle matched 1,536 small pages. Use this to separate the economics of exact answers from witness disclosure. **Still to prove and measure:** native prefix-local weighted interning, edit-local updates, exact probability mass and dominance under rewritten weights. The support-only quotient cannot justify merging different weight distributions.
