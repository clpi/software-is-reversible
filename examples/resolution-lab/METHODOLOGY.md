# Structural search for high-consequence compositions

## What is represented
Use the canonical append-only graph as authority. A search cut identifies its events and semantics. An edge carries a set of jointly required facts and a set of produced facts; incidence, current intent, action candidates, costs, obscurity, and proof status are views. Keep disagreements and unknowns explicit. A chat line can change many derived decisions without becoming evidence for an unobserved event. The prototype implements a scoped, offline counterpart: graph append and candidate search in one fixture, exact tensor replay and an action sandbox in separate fixtures. It does **not** yet persist these three as one production Idol graph.

Represent a conjecture or design candidate as a typed rule or plan over an admitted subgraph, with an independently checkable enabling predicate. Generate single choices, AND-bundles, competing implementations, projection refinements, codecs, reuse/cold variants, and stop/continue pairs. Use structural identity to merge duplicate recipes without dropping witness coverage. Register both baseline and ambitious alternatives before downselection, including ideas proposed by an outer search.

## What is optimized
Do not score “groundbreaking” as fact. Keep separate typed axes: checked downstream consequences, newly provable joint consequences, canonical search coverage, evidence needed to certify, modeled/measured cost and payload size, and inventory-conditioned novelty. An idea is structurally new within the searched inventory only when no matching checked witness is found **and** the named inventory is covered. Unsearched is not new. Quantify downstream loss on removing a premise, complementarities on adding bundles, and reuse on incremental prefixes. Record units, objective direction, legal domain, and oracle. Reweighting changes the choice, not the frontier; replacing axes or eligibility changes the query and invalidates relevant certificates. Search itself has measurable compute, latency, and retained-byte costs.

For a retained result report its reachable and actually newly derived consequences separately. The factored solver only prunes histories sharing a sufficient future interface, with a lawful continuation and componentwise no-worse costs. With additive costs the numeric dominance comparison survives that continuation. This preserves the frontier of vectors; distinct equal-vector identities may remain valuable as witnesses and should be stored or represented by a reconstructible fiber. The Lean file proves a more general context-specific cover argument and complementarity (two sterile single additions, one productive joint addition). The browser fixture uses JS in the reference search and delegates checked primitive comparisons to real Idol-generated Wasm. This is not a formally refined implementation of every Lean result.

## Negative controls and empirical gates
1. Rebuild the cut from scratch, compare incremental and cold output after every append, and perturb delivery order. Test both same and changed future interfaces. A newly valid continuation that needs discarded evidence forces replay.
2. Compare independent exhaustive enumeration against the search, not the search against itself. Record every admitted coordinate and candidate domain. Register held-out candidates to test whether a narrower frontier survives the expanded domain.
3. For token replay, keep exact cumulative mass. A deterministic tie rule only picks among equal qualified solutions; the early stop rule must bound every remaining prefix, not just cross an action threshold. Budget exhaustion is unresolved. Compute projection fibers independently of eligibility and refine until the requested ambiguity gate is met or report the missing resolution.
4. For storage, compare actual encoded bytes plus decoder and recovery cost. Lossless equivalence is not smaller storage: the sample sparse encoding stores fewer explicit entries but is larger as JSON. Never infer a universally minimum compressor from a registered candidate minimum.
5. For reversal, inspect fresh pre-, post- and restored states. A changed fresh state refuses replay. A pseudo-inverse, similar output, or compensating external action is not exact reversal.
6. Separate measured cost from synthetic operation counts, and byte-replay from claims about a model's original intent. Repeat paired trials with workload, host, program and graph cut pinned. Include no-op / failure controls and publish losing results.
7. Before any production actuator, native compilation and replay must be traced to exact current source, receipt checker, policy and prerequisites. A demo's local state machine cannot certify a browser, process, or remote action.

## Verified first slice
- Search: 124 independent Cartesian checks over two architectures, two profiles and 31 nonempty axis subsets. On the full fixture 62 resolved tuples yield 18 frontier vectors. Candidate extensions drop 100→48. At a compatible append, 3 stages survive and 27 rather than 48 extensions are new. Held-out work dominates 14 of 20 points in the narrowed profile.
- Tensor: 400 independent exhaustive comparisons. The default constructed tensor produces exact sequence [1,2] at 8/25; greedy produces [0,0,2] at 1/5. Stopping uses a strict bound; the browser example refines the projection fiber from 3 to 1 before dispatching a sandbox flip. No model checkpoint or live inference.
- Reversal: 128 length-four sandbox traces and 3,584 mismatched-fresh-state refusals. Scoped reversal landed separately; its 24,576-plan synthetic sweep is not the website's planner benchmark.
- Native: two equal 3,430-byte artifacts on the retained compiler, 4,761 checked signed comparator pairs, and finite action inversion/refusal controls. No WASI authority delegated. Native selection and real platform control remain separate, unqualified work.

The strongest next falsifiable step is a context-sensitive symbolic closure of experimentally collected evidence: compare proposal-only, incremental factored search, and exact enumeration over the same append prefixes; hold out real interaction venues, novel compositions and failed rollback paths. Expand the candidate domain and keep old certificates addressable rather than extending the meaning of a previous optimum.

## Prior art and the measured tradeoff

Multiobjective path labels are established ([Martins, 1984](https://doi.org/10.1016/0377-2217(84)90077-8)), and the preservation of Bellman's principle under a Pareto product is established ([Saule and Giegerich, 2015](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4491898/)). Neither is a novel claim here. The candidate worth taking forward is the combination of a **refutable future-interface contract** with **append-local certificate reuse**, held-out domain expansion, and distinctions between equal-objective but semantically distinct witness identities. Standalone historical novelty remains unestablished.

`benchmark.mjs` timed 360 cold-cache search/rank/certify executions on four registered profiles, three engines, 15 retained trials per pair, and two separate processes. Rotated order; graph parsing excluded; every frontier checked against exact enumeration. On the full five-axis cut the two process medians (ms) were: exhaustive 2.7474/2.5118, factored reference 2.4962/2.2749, factored with Wasm scalar calls 2.7066/2.3109. The reference won ~9% on this cut in both processes. Node→Wasm dispatch in the comparison inner loop cost more in both of those runs; it is not a latency win. On the narrow control the factored reference changed from a win to essentially a tie/slight loss. This means the next serious candidate is **fused native frontier calculation**, not a claim that replacing each scalar call improves performance. These timings cover one host and four projections of a constructed graph, not a cross-architecture production conclusion.

The incidence table is derived with two role bits per edge/node pair. A node in both premise and conclusion must not cancel to zero. Reconstruction of both memberships is tested. The actual native comparator, not a mock, participated in 124 checked graph/profile/axis combinations across two append cuts.

**Fused experiment.** A second real Idol binary evaluates all active oriented coordinates in one call. Two matching builds, 1,830,519 exhaustive small comparisons, 20,000 held-out signed comparisons and refusal controls passed. Direct JS, scalar Wasm, fused Wasm with input conversion, and fused prepacked were measured twice with matched pairs. Fused-with-conversion only beats JS on tie-rich inputs; prepacked is the faster inner loop but its measured one-pass packing cost dwarfs the isolated gain. Keep it inspectable as a browser alternative, not the default. The next frontier must include reuse count, retained bytes and conversion amortization, not just comparison time.
